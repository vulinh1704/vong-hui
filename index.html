Vòng Hụi PUSD

A hụi is a rotating savings circle (ROSCA): every round each member pays in, and one member takes the whole pot. Here the pot is held by a CashScript covenant on Bitcoin Cash instead of an organiser, so nobody can run off with it.

Every button below builds a real BCH transaction with the project's SDK and broadcasts it to a simulated node, which checks it in the BCH virtual machine (2026 rules) against the real covenant. Refused transactions show the covenant's own error.

The rules (enforced by the covenant)

  1. 4 members in a fixed order: member k takes the pot in round k.
  2. Each round every member pays 10,000 sat. Round 1 costs 20,000: 10,000 plus 10,000 of collateral.
  3. Once all 4 have paid, anyone may release the pot (40,000 sat minus the miner fee), but only to that round's member. No signature, no organiser key.
  4. If a round misses its deadline, anyone may cancel it: each member gets back what they paid that round plus their collateral. A member who already received a pot and stopped paying loses the collateral, split among the members who have not received yet.
  5. Money can only ever leave to the 4 member addresses fixed when the circle was created.

About this simulation

  • Network: CashScript's MockNetworkProvider, in memory. Nothing leaves your browser; keys are throwaway test keys.
  • Each round lasts 6 blocks here. The mock node checks scripts and lock times like a real node, but not coin age (BIP68), so the page enforces the covenant's 1-block grace period itself before the first refund.
  • Transaction sizes and fees are real: 1 sat/byte, as a BCH node would relay them.
